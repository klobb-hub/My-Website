🧠 Memory Match

Find every matching pair. Choose a difficulty, flip two cards at a time, and try to finish in as few moves and as little time as possible. Have fun! :D

How to play

  1. Choose a difficulty to show the board.
  2. Click a card to reveal its symbol.
  3. Click a second card to look for a match.
  4. Matching cards stay visible.
  5. Mismatched cards turn back over.
  6. Find all the pairs to win.
