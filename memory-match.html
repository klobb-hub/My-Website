🧠 Memory Match

Find all matching pairs as quickly as possible. Flip two cards at a time, remember where each symbol is, and try to beat your personal best.

How to play

  1. Click a card to reveal it.
  2. Click a second card.
  3. If they match, they stay visible.
  4. If they do not match, they are hidden again.
  5. Find all 18 pairs to win.
